# 宇宙泰坦 / Cosmic Titan 隐私政策

开发者／运营者：胡冬生 
联系邮箱：hudongsheng356@gmail.com

宇宙泰坦是一款在 Mac 本机进行光学设计与分析的应用。当前版本无需创建账户。应用不向开发者服务器传送工程、光学参数、导入帧或计算结果；不内置广告、用户追踪或第三方分析 SDK。

工程与导出文件保存在用户通过系统文件选择器指定的位置。语言偏好和自定义库等应用设置保存在本机。用户可以通过应用和 macOS 文件管理功能管理自己的文件。如果选择 iCloud Drive 或其他同步目录，相应文件同步由该服务及用户设置控制。

应用仅访问用户选择的文件，用于打开、保存和导入导出。核心计算不需要网络连接。主动点击外部参考链接后，浏览器访问相应第三方网站，其隐私政策适用。

如果您主动通过电子邮件联系支持，我们会收到您发送的联系信息和内容，仅用于回复及处理问题。请不要发送无关的敏感信息。支持渠道及其数据保留安排须由运营者在发布前确认；如实际做法不同，应更新本政策与商店申报。

# Cosmic Titan Privacy Policy (complete before publication)

Developer/operator: Dongsheng Hu
Contact: hudongsheng356@gmail.com

Cosmic Titan performs optical design and analysis locally on your Mac. The current version requires no account and does not send your projects, imported frames, optical parameters or calculation results to developer-operated servers. It includes no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK.

Project and export files are saved to locations you select. Preferences and custom libraries are stored locally. Files placed in iCloud Drive or another synchronized folder are handled by that service according to your settings and its privacy terms.

The app accesses user-selected files for opening, saving, importing and exporting. Core calculations work without a network connection. If you choose an external reference link, your browser visits a third-party website governed by its own privacy policy.

If you contact support by email, we receive the contact details and content you send and use them to respond to your request. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information. The operator must confirm the support provider and retention practices before publishing this policy and update the policy and store disclosures if actual practices differ.

For privacy questions, contact the email above.
