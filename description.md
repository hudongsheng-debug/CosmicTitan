让光，构成宇宙。

宇宙泰坦是面向光学学习、教学与研究探索的 Mac 光学设计与分析工作台。将透镜、反射镜、光纤、分束器、滤光片与探测器组合成光路，在可视化环境中探索参数与结果的关系。

搭建你的光学系统
• 通过节点拖拽与端口连线组织光学路径，编辑传播距离和元件参数。
• 在三维实物装配视图中调整元件位置与方向，使用对齐、锁定和替换辅助操作。
• 保存工程、自定义器件与可复用子装配，从已有配置开始新的设计。

观察光的传播
• 探索球面与非球面处方、Snell 几何追迹，以及选定模型下的光斑、波前和衍射。
• 在装配中使用虚拟观察面检查几何光线截面与功率。
• 结合交叉色散光谱仪、Lyot 日冕仪、探测器读出和参数扫描工具，比较不同设计条件。
• 在支持的场景中探索 ABCD 网络、非顺序鬼像与二维角谱传播。

深入光纤与集成光子
• 探索标量光纤模式、长周期光纤光栅两模耦合，以及预设锥区的标量 BPM 传播。
• 分析理想 AWG 的端口光谱，导出 TE/TM 分块复数 S 矩阵。
• 使用六边形 IFU 几何分析，或导入外部标定数据进行谱迹联合提取与光谱立方体重建。

保留分析依据
• 查看各模块的模型假设、适用范围，以及支持模块的能量账本与数值检查。
• 将支持的结果导出为 CSV、JSON 或 FITS，衔接后续分析。
• 核心计算在本机完成，无需注册账户。

宇宙泰坦包含几何、标量及理想化研究模型。各模块的求解范围不同，部分实验独立于装配光路；它不提供覆盖所有模块的全矢量电磁、完整非顺序杂散光或制造级仪器验证。请结合模块说明和独立实验验证解释结果。

适用于 macOS 14 或更高版本。提供简体中文和英文界面；用户自定义名称与导入资料保留原文。



Let light shape your ideas.

Cosmic Titan is a Mac workspace for optical learning, teaching and research exploration. Combine lenses, mirrors, fibers, beam splitters, filters and detectors to investigate how design choices affect optical results.

Build optical systems
• Organize paths with draggable nodes and optical port connections.
• Adjust component positions and orientations in a 3D assembly view, with alignment, locking and replacement tools.
• Save projects, custom components and reusable subassemblies.

Explore propagation
• Investigate spherical and aspherical prescriptions, Snell ray tracing, and model-specific spot, wavefront and diffraction analyses.
• Use virtual observation planes to inspect geometric ray cross sections and power within an assembly.
• Compare settings with cross-dispersed spectrometer, Lyot coronagraph, detector-readout and parameter-scan tools.
• Explore ABCD networks, non-sequential ghosts and 2D angular-spectrum propagation within supported models.

Study fibers and photonics
• Explore scalar fiber modes, two-mode long-period fiber grating coupling and scalar BPM in preset taper structures.
• Calculate ideal AWG port spectra and export TE/TM block complex scattering matrices.
• Explore hexagonal IFU geometry, or import external calibration data for joint spectral extraction and cube reconstruction.

Keep the assumptions visible
• Consult model scope and assumptions, with energy accounting and numerical checks in supported modules.
• Export supported results as CSV, JSON or FITS for further analysis.
• Run core calculations locally, without creating an account.

The app includes geometric, scalar and idealized research models. Solver coverage varies by module, and some experiments operate independently of the assembly path. It does not provide comprehensive full-vector electromagnetics, complete non-sequential stray-light analysis or manufacturing-grade instrument validation. Interpret results using each module's documentation and independent validation.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Available in Simplified Chinese and English. Custom names and imported material retain their original language.
